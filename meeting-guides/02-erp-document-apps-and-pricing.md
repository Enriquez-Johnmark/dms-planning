# Meeting Guide — ERP Document Apps and Pricing

**Duration:** about 30 minutes. **Detail:** [Section 02](../sections/02-market-and-competitor-research.md).

## Meeting Objective

Decide **how the Documents app is priced** and agree the selling message.

## Why This Matters to the Business

Price and pitch decide whether Documents helps sell Officetul or becomes a hard extra to sell.

## Plain-Language Explanation

- **Competitors include documents for free.** Xero, QuickBooks and Zoho don't charge extra for storing and attaching receipts. They only charge extra for **AI that reads a receipt and fills in the amount for you**.
- **Odoo, the ERP most like Officetul, charges for it.** Its Documents app is only in the paid edition, about ₱650 per user per month, and its OCR is charged per document.
- **Our running cost is small.** Storing and searching a typical client's documents costs us roughly ₱50–260 a month.
- **Nobody offers these:** searching inside every scan with no per-page fee, an "official version" marker, or Philippine-ready document types such as BIR 2307 and Official Receipt. **That's our pitch.**

> **Update 2026-10-18:** the project owner deferred the pricing decision until the server and operating cost estimate is ready (D-018). For now, use this meeting to gather Q2 (how Officetul is priced today) and the senior partner's first reaction to Q1 and Q3. Don't decide.

## Questions to Discuss

1. **Included or add-on?** Do we include Documents in the Officetul subscription (recommended), or sell it as a separate add-on of roughly ₱500–1,600 a month per company?
2. **How is Officetul priced today?** Per user, per company, or by tier? What margin do you need?
3. **Is the pitch right?** "Documents included: search inside every scan, attach files to any transaction, always know the official version. No per-page fees."
4. **Storage:** is a generous allowance per company, with paid top-ups for heavy users, acceptable?

## Options and Trade-offs

| Option | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| Included | Easiest to sell; beats Odoo | No separate revenue line |
| Add-on | Extra revenue | A second thing to sell; competitors include it |
