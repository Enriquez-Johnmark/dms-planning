# Meeting Guide — Document Types

**Duration:** about 20 minutes. **Detail:** [Section 03](../sections/03-philippine-sme-workflows.md) and the [catalogue](../appendices/document-type-catalogue.md).

## Meeting Objective

Agree **which document types the Documents app knows out of the box**, and whether to pay for a short legal and tax check.

## Why This Matters to the Business

Ready-made Philippine document types make the app feel built for our clients from day one. Getting a BIR rule wrong would embarrass us in front of accountants.

## Plain-Language Explanation

- **The BIR changed receipts in 2024.** The **Invoice** is now the proof of sale. Official receipts only prove payment. Our app will say "Invoice" and "Payment Receipt", but typing "OR" still finds them.
- **About 22 standard types for every client:** invoices, BIR 2307, tax returns, permits, DTI, SEC, contracts, employee files, bonds and more.
- **Accounting firms get an extra set** (engagement letters, client submissions, working papers). It's just setup, so it can come with the first version.
- **Law firms and contractors get their own packs later.** Those need new features (court deadlines; drawing revisions), not just new labels.
- **Some papers must stay on paper**, e.g. manual books of accounts, signed contracts and notarized documents. The app records where the original is kept.

## Questions to Discuss

1. Is the standard list right for our clients? Anything missing?
2. Do many of our clients do accounting, law or construction work? That affects which pack comes first after the MVP.
3. Can we get a Philippine **lawyer and tax adviser** for one or two short sessions? Who, and what budget?
