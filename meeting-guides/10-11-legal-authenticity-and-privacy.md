# Meeting Guide — Legal: Authenticity and Data Privacy

**Duration:** about 30 minutes. **Detail:** [Section 10](../sections/10-authenticity-and-certified-true-copies.md) and [Section 11](../sections/11-philippine-legal-and-regulatory-research.md). **Questions for professionals:** [legal-and-tax-questions.md](../questionnaires/legal-and-tax-questions.md).

## Meeting Objective

Agree **what we promise clients**, and **which company tasks** must be done before a client stores documents with us.

## Why This Matters to the Business

A wrong promise ("court-admissible", "certified true copy") can cost a client a case and expose us to claims. Missing privacy paperwork can bring NPC penalties, now that we hold clients' IDs, tax and payroll records.

## Plain-Language Explanation

- **A scan is a copy, not the original.** Courts generally accept an accurate copy, but if anyone questions it, the paper matters. That's why we never tell clients to throw away originals.
- **We can't issue "Certified True Copies".** Only the government office holding a record, or a notary, can. Later we may offer a "Copy issued from [Company] records", signed by the client's own officer, with a clear disclaimer.
- **Words we never use:** "court-admissible", "tamper-proof", "BIR-compliant", "certified true copy", "go paperless".
- **Under privacy law we're the client's "processor".** We store their data on their instructions. That means:
  - a standard privacy contract (data processing addendum);
  - telling clients **within 24 hours** if something goes wrong, because they have only 72 hours to report to the National Privacy Commission;
  - being registered with the NPC as a processor, with a named Data Protection Officer;
  - MFA for access to sensitive files.
- **Hosting in Singapore is allowed.** We just have to say where the data is.

## Questions to Discuss

1. Is the company **already registered with the NPC**, and does it have a **Data Protection Officer** for Officetul today?
2. Who will own the **pre-launch privacy tasks** (contract template, sub-processor list, incident procedure)?
3. Do you agree with the **"words we never use"** list for sales and marketing?
4. Who is our **lawyer and tax adviser** for the review sessions (D-027)?
