# Meeting Guide — ISO Certification

**Duration:** about 20 minutes. **Detail:** [Section 04](../sections/04-iso-certification-strategy.md).

> **Owner: the senior partner** (D-028). ISO certification is a **future goal**. The project owner has handed the choice of standard and timing to the senior partner. It does not block the Documents app, which is built to be ISO-ready either way (D-011).

## Meeting Objective

Choose **what we want ISO-certified, and when.**

## Why This Matters to the Business

ISO certification costs money every year and takes staff time. The right certificate wins trust and tenders; the wrong one is wasted effort.

## Plain-Language Explanation

- **Software can't be ISO-certified. A company can.** An independent, accredited auditor checks how our *company* runs things (policies, risks, people, processes) and issues a certificate to the company. We could then truthfully say "our company is ISO 27001 certified for running Officetul". We could never say "Officetul is ISO certified".
- **The two certificates that make sense for us:**
  - **ISO 27001 (information security):** "you can trust us with your documents and data." This is the best fit for a company that stores clients' documents.
  - **ISO 9001 (quality):** "we run consistent processes." It mostly helps in tenders. The new 2026 edition just came out.
- **It's a yearly programme, not a one-time purchase:** audits, then a yearly check, then renewal every 3 years. Practitioners say 6–12 months from start to certificate for a small company (an estimate). We found no published Philippine price; government budgets of ₱400,000–469,000 over 3 years cover auditor fees only.
- **The Documents app helps** by keeping our policies, versions, approvals and access logs organised, which is evidence the auditor asks for. **It can't do the certification for us.**

## Questions to Discuss

1. **Which do you want?**
   - (C) **ISO 27001 for our company, covering Officetul** (recommended)
   - (A) ISO 9001
   - both
   - neither for now
2. **When?** Recommended: start when a client or tender asks for it, or right after the Documents pilot, whichever comes first.
3. **Has any client or tender asked** for an ISO certificate?
4. **Which company name** would hold the certificate?
5. **May we request 2–3 quotes** from accredited certification bodies?

## Options and Trade-offs

| Option | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| 27001 when triggered | Strong trust story, timed to need | Lead time if a deal needs it suddenly |
| 27001 now | Ready early | Competes with building the Documents app |
| 9001 | Tenders | Doesn't answer data-security questions |
| Neither for now | No cost | Weaker sales story to security-minded clients |
