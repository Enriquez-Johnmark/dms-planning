One row per source cited in section documents. Research notes in [`../research/`](../research/) keep their own full source lists. A source enters this register when a section relies on it.

**Source tiers** (most to least authoritative):
1. Legislation, government, regulators, courts, standards bodies
2. Official product docs, vendor pricing, security advisories
3. Peer-reviewed or credible independent research
4. Industry analysis, independent reviews, news
5. Practitioner reports, community discussions (for operational insight only)

**"Excerpt"** means the site blocked automated fetching and the figure came from a search-engine excerpt of the official page. Re-check it in a browser before quoting it externally.
