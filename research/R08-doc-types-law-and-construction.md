# R08: Document types for law firms and construction contractors

**Question:** Which documents do Philippine law firms and construction or project-based firms handle? Which should be MVP presets, and which belong in later industry packs?
**Researched:** 2026-10-18. **Every source was accessed on 2026-10-18.** Claims cite `[S#]`; URLs are in section 6.

**Labels:** **VF** = Verified fact (read in a primary text or an official site). **SEC** = Secondary (law firm, news, consultancy, wiki). **EST** = Estimate or industry practice, not a rule. **OQ** = Open question. **REC** = Recommendation.

**Limits on this research:**
- The web-search budget ran out partway through. After that, only known URLs could be fetched.
- These pages were blocked or not machine-readable: the DPWH copy of the PD 1096 IRR (bot wall), the GPPB IRR text of RA 12009 (only the approving resolution was read), the PCAB licensing rules PDF (a scanned image), and the CIAP site (the domain doesn't resolve).
- The CPRA and the 2004 notarial rules were read in ChanRobles reproductions. The Supreme Court's own copies were not read.

## 3. What a generic MVP must not get wrong (REC)

1. **File size and formats.**
   - SC e-filing allows up to 100 MB per file [S14]. Drawing sets and scanned evidence exceed common 25 MB limits [S18].
   - **REC:** accept at least 100 MB per file (ideally 250 MB+ for drawing sets) and stream large PDFs in the viewer.
   - Never let a document type accept ZIP as its only form. Courts reject archives [S15][S18].
2. **Keep the original file byte-for-byte.**
   - The scanned image beats the OCR text in court [S15]. Integrity evidence decides admissibility [S9].
   - **REC:** store the original unchanged, plus a SHA-256 hash and upload or capture metadata. Keep OCR text as a separate layer. Never "optimise" the stored original.
   - Exports must not be password-protected [S15].
   - Offer **PDF/A export** (e-filing [S14], e-notarization [S6]).
3. **"Official version" ≠ "current revision".**
   - A drawing has a revision (A, B, 0, 1…) and an issue status. Typical statuses are For Information, For Approval, For Construction, As-Built and Superseded [S33][S34][S35] (EST: no PH rule).
   - **REC:** the MVP's version model needs a free-text **revision label** separate from the internal version number. Also add a **status** field on the document type, so "For Construction, Rev C" can be found in one click and superseded sheets are clearly marked.
4. **Privilege and SPI must override convenience.**
   - **Search, OCR snippets, previews and activity-log text must respect permissions.** A user who can't open a privileged matter must not see its OCR text in search results.
   - Add a per-document **"Privileged / Confidential" flag**, and default matter folders to restricted access [S1][S10].
   - Court-proceeding data is SPI [S10], which counts toward NPC registration thresholds (see R01).
5. **"Keep paper original" plus physical location, not a scan-and-shred culture.**
   - Notarial books, signed and sealed plans, logbooks, bonds and Certificates of Completion all have paper originals that a rule requires [S3][S24].
   - **REC:** add a preset field **"Paper original kept? Where? Custodian"** on every type. This matches the company's never-destroy policy.
6. **Custody can leave the firm.**
   - Client files are turned over on termination (s.56) [S1]. Notarial registers go to the Executive Judge (R.VI s.5(b)) [S3].
   - **REC:** add an **"export matter bundle + record handover"** action (who, when, what). Allow "original surrendered" without deleting the digital working copy.
   - Whether the firm may keep copies after turnover → **OQ** for a lawyer.
7. **Retention clocks start at events, not upload dates.**
   - Construction risk runs 15 years from completion, then 10 more to sue (art. 1723) [S19]. Contract actions prescribe in 10 years (art. 1144) [S19].
   - **REC:** store event dates now (completion, final acceptance, matter closed) so the next release's retention rules can count from them. **No default auto-delete.**
8. **Many-to-many links are essential.** One change order touches the project, owner, subcontractor and sales invoice. One court order touches the matter and the client. One bond touches the project and the surety.
9. **Expiry fields from day one,** even though reminders come later. They are needed for PCAB (30 June) [S28], bonds and securities [S20], insurance, IDs, and ENP or notarial commissions.
10. **Notarial reference fields** (Doc No., Page No., Book No., Series of) appear on every notarized PH document, including LGU forms [S24]. **REC:** make them an optional field group on the generic "Notarized document" type, and make them searchable.

## 5. Open questions

**For a Philippine lawyer:**
1. Is there **any minimum period for keeping client files** after a matter closes (CPRA, IBP guidance, case law)? What do firms do in practice? [L2]
2. After a **s.56 turnover**, may the firm keep a digital copy of the client's file (for defence against later complaints), and does the attorney's lien cover digital copies? [S1]
3. Under R.VI s.5(b), do "notarial records" delivered to the Executive Judge include the notary's **retained original copies of contracts** (s.2(d)), or only the register? [S3]
4. Retention duties for **ENPs** (electronic notarial book, session recordings), and does OCA Circular 61-2025 add any? [S7]
5. Does hosting privileged matter files with a cloud processor (Officetul) need **client consent** or specific contract terms under CPRA s.27–28 and the DPA?
6. Do law firms that form or manage companies for clients (a covered AMLA service) register with the AMLC and keep 5-year KYC records? [S11][S13]
7. Confirm the official A.M. No. 25-09-16-SC text: the 100 MB limit, the naming rule, and whether an **electronic signature** standard applies [S14][S15].

**Construction and government:**
